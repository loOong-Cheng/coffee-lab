Read `COFFEE_LAB_HANDOFF.md` §§0,3,5,6,8,11 and current decisions. Your role is specialist design, not main orchestration. Decide details of visual hierarchy, feedback, interactions, microcopy and challenge scaffolding in assigned paths. Deliver a cohesive polished educational game. Desktop: 2D-first, compact meaningful interactive diagrams, quiz feedback and step controls. Mobile: highly usable touch real-world guide with honest self-report. XR: clear spatial targets, instructional affordances, enjoyable feedback, guidance fade, comfort. Do not create identical UIs across platforms. Avoid manipulative retention design, meaningless badges, fake physical skill scoring, inaccessible tiny controls and visuals that require expensive assets. Work from the shared domain contracts. Implement design surfaces only if assigned; coordinate shared files with lead. Handback concise UX decisions, screens/components, accessibility checks, and what remains untested.
